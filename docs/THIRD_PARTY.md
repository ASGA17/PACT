# Third-party materials

Policy texts and reference benchmark data originate from their respective providers. Their applicable upstream terms remain in effect; inclusion does not grant additional rights.

The ten reference datasets are AdvBench, DAN, JBB-Behaviors, LLM-Fuzz, MaliciousInstruct, MasterKey, AIR-Bench, HarmBench, SORRY-Bench, and SOS-Bench.

Model weights are not included. Python dependencies are installed separately. This repository does not assign a blanket license to third-party datasets or policy texts.
